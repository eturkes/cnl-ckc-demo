# m5u2 — query-local cap-2 assumption evaluator

Unit: M5 u2 · tier `kernel` · flags `oracle` · Depends u1 (DONE, `e71486e`).
Base SHA every downstream artifact runs against = **`e71486e`**.

## Problem

`tools/kb/proof.mjs` cuts every shipped `clinical_advice/3` goal before resolution and
synthesizes leaves from build-time `site/2` records, so `resolve/3` is never reached by
any shipped question (ledger S1/S3). u1 shipped the records a real derivation needs —
48 `clinical_rule/3`, 346 `clinical_premise/4`, 48 `clinical_gate/4` over 686 content
sites. u2 ships the evaluator that consumes them: a **query-local assumption
meta-interpreter at cap 2**, writing nothing to the world.

## Scope boundary

u2 ships the **evaluator**; it does not rewire a consumer.

| surface | owner |
| --- | --- |
| `tools/kb/proof.mjs` — assumption MI + clinical driver | **u2** |
| `src/questions/**`, `session.ts` answer assembly | u3 |
| removal of the `clinical_advice` proof dispatch, `session.ts:441` depth, typed decoder branches | u4 |
| `ProvenanceLadder.svelte` premise display | u5 |

**The `clinical_advice` cut STAYS in u2.** Removing it alone yields 0/7 proofs
(`resolve/3` whitelists nine `guideline_*` predicates; `session.ts:441` hard-codes
interpreter depth 1) — so removing it before u4 breaks the shipped proof path and the
gate with it. The roadmap's word "Replace" is executed across u2+u4: u2 lands the
replacement mechanism, u4 lands the removal of what it replaces. Scope clarification,
not a scope change.

## Design rulings (MAIN)

- **D1 — one interpreter, not two.** `derive/4` → `derive/5`, threading a query-local
  assumption list; `derive_conjunction/5` → `/6`. `mi(G,D,P) :- derive(G,D,[],P,proved)`
  and `mi_limited(G,D) :- derive(G,D,[],_,limit)` keep their arity and their behaviour,
  so the shipped proof RPC is untouched (P1). A parallel second interpreter was rejected:
  it duplicates `resolve/3`, the NAF branch and the conjunction states, and the two would
  drift.
- **D2 — assumption leaf = `assumption(H)`.** `H` is the grounded premise literal, whose
  `'$clinical_hypothetical'(Doc,S,N)` skolems already name its hypothetical origin, so no
  extra label argument ships. The clause sits **before** the depth cap and before
  `resolve/3` and commits with a cut, so an assumed literal never also resolves against a
  KB clause and never emits a `line(L)`.
- **D3 — cap 2, declared in the image** as `clinical_depth(2)`. Measured: cap 1 proves
  47/48 sentences, cap 2 and cap 3 both prove 48/48, so 2 is the least sufficient cap.
  `clinical_derive/4` applies the ruled cap; `clinical_derive/5` takes an explicit cap,
  which is what lets a test prove cap 1 insufficient.
- **D4 — heads come from the stored gate body, never from executing the gate.**
  `clinical_gate/4`'s body is `clinical_use(L1,H1),…,clinical_use(Ln,Hn),clinical_rule(…)`
  with every head ground and inline — u1 chose that shape precisely so `clause/3`
  enumerates sites without executing them. Executing the gate is circular: `clinical_use`
  calls `call(Body)`, which demands the very premises the evaluator supplies.
  `gate_heads/2` walks the conjunction and stops at the trailing `clinical_rule/3`.
- **D5 — the rule is released last.** `clinical_derive/5` binds `Rule` from
  `clinical_rule/3` only after `derive_all/4` succeeds. That ordering is what makes the
  fragment data gated rather than looked up, and it is what P4 erases against.
- **D6 — everything u2 ships lives in `tools/kb/proof.mjs`.** `PROOF_SOURCE` is appended
  **last** in `payloadSource`, after the payload and after u1's clinical helper, so no u2
  edit can move a content-site line or a `clinical_gate/4` line list (P2). u1's producer
  stays byte-frozen.
- **D7 — no writes.** No `assertz`, `asserta`, `retract`, `erase` or `dynamic` in shipped
  source. Test-side erasure runs inside `snapshot/1` on a test-declared dynamic
  predicate; the shipped image must still refuse a write.

## Emitted surface

Added to `PROOF_SOURCE`. Names are project-idiomatic, never the probes' `m5_*`.

```prolog
assumed(H,[A|_]) :- H = A.                    % unification, so an assumption may bind
assumed(H,[_|T]) :- assumed(H,T).
derive(H,_,As,[assumption(H)],proved) :- assumed(H,As), !.   % before the cap, before resolve
derive_all([],_,_,[]).
derive_all([H|T],D,As,P) :- derive(H,D,As,PH,proved), derive_all(T,D,As,PT), app(PH,PT,P).
gate_heads((clinical_use(_,H),B),[H|T]) :- !, gate_heads(B,T).
gate_heads(_,[]).
clinical_depth(2).
clinical_context(Doc,S,As) :- findall(L,clinical_premise(Doc,S,_,L),As).
clinical_derive(Doc,S,Rule,P) :- clinical_depth(D), clinical_derive(Doc,S,D,Rule,P).
clinical_derive(Doc,S,D,Rule,P) :- clause(clinical_gate(Doc,S,_,_),Body,_), gate_heads(Body,Heads),
    clinical_context(Doc,S,As), derive_all(Heads,D,As,P), clinical_rule(Doc,S,Rule).
```

## Predicates

P1-P5 are the roadmap's acceptance clauses; P6-P10 are the obligations they imply.
Every figure below is MAIN-measured on a candidate implementation against the real
generated image — reproduce with `.scratch/m5u2/`, which is regenerable and not committed.

- **P1 — the shipped proof path is unchanged.** `mi/3` and `mi_limited/2` keep their
  arity and their output for every existing case; the `derive(clinical_advice(...))`
  dispatch still answers; every pre-existing test stays green. u2 adds capability, it
  does not alter a shipped answer or proof byte.
- **P2 — no line moved.** All 48 `clinical_gate/4` line lists and the 686-line content
  site set are byte-identical to `e71486e`. `PROOF_SOURCE` is last in the payload, so
  this holds by construction and is checked, not assumed.
- **P3 — cap 2 is the ruled and the least sufficient depth.** At cap 2 all **48**
  content sentences derive and all **12** documents derive in full. At cap 1 exactly
  **47/48** derive; the single failure is **`cdc2022-opioid-rec05:5`**, the corpus's one
  NAF antecedent. Cap 3 adds nothing (48/48). `clinical_depth(2)` is declared in the
  image and is what `clinical_derive/4` applies.
- **P4 — every cited clause independently controls the derivation.** Erasing any one of
  the **686** cited clauses inside `snapshot/1` makes its sentence's `clinical_derive`
  fail — **686/686, zero survivors**, campaign wall time **4.8 s**. Failing releases
  neither `Rule` nor `Proof`, so answer and proof change together (D5). After the whole
  campaign all 48 sentences re-derive, proving the snapshot rolled back.
- **P5 — both negative controls hold at document grain.** Premises withheld
  (`derive_all(Heads,2,[],_)`): **0 of 12** documents yield a full recommendation.
  Schema erased (every `guideline_*` clause retracted in a snapshot): **0 of 48**
  sentences and 0 of 12 documents derive.
  **Grain matters and the sentence grain is not the predicate.** Under withheld premises
  **2 of 48** sentences still derive — one in `cdc2022-opioid-rec10`, one in
  `cdc2022-opioid-rec12`. Those are u1's two `true`-antecedent gates, which carry no
  premises, so withholding premises correctly changes nothing for them. Both documents
  have 3 content sentences and neither completes. A test asserting 0/48 here is wrong.
- **P6 — assumptions are honest.** Every hypothetical premise appears as
  `assumption(Literal)` and never as `node(line(L),…)`; no assumption leaf carries a
  source line; every `node(line(L),H,_)` in a `clinical_derive` proof names a line in
  that sentence's `clinical_gate/4` list, and their union over a document is exactly
  that document's cited set.
- **P7 — no world mutation.** Shipped `PROOF_SOURCE` contains no `assertz`, `asserta`,
  `retract`, `retractall`, `erase` or `dynamic`. After any number of derivations against
  the shipped image, every `guideline_*` predicate is still static and its clause count
  is unchanged. A direct `assertz` against a schema predicate still raises a permission
  error.
- **P8 — inside the proof budget.** Worst synchronous `clinical_derive` step **8.5 ms**
  measured at caps 1/2/3, against `PROOF_BUDGET_MAX.wallClockMs` 1000; no sentence
  reaches `call_with_inference_limit`'s 100000 or `call_with_depth_limit`'s 100. The 4
  gates u1 handed over as natively unresolvable (`rec01:3`, `rec02:3`, `rec02:8`,
  `rec05:4`, each exhausting 200000 inferences natively) all terminate here — the depth
  cap is what discharges u1's handed constraint.
- **P9 — gate green.** `pnpm gate` rc 0 from a clean `kb/generated`, u2's suite inside
  `pnpm test`, u1's 20 cases still green.
- **P10 — budget declared.** The payload/PVM/QLF byte delta u2 adds is measured and
  recorded, never estimated.

## Measured facts this unit adds

- **3,930 assumption leaves**, not the roadmap's 3,882. The 3,882 figure came from the
  probe's `m5_prepare`, which computed premises at runtime and emitted none for a
  sentence whose antecedent already proved natively. u1's records ship every positive
  literal as a premise, so the shipped evaluator emits one leaf per premise use.
- **A permission error does not reach `.once()` as `$error`.** `assertz` against a static
  schema predicate prints `No permission to modify static procedure` to real stderr,
  outside the `printErr` drain, and the JS call returns normally. Read the refusal from
  `catch/3` inside Prolog. A JS-side `try/catch` around `.once()` reports the write as
  ALLOWED and is a false negative.
- A helper clause asserted as `assertz((Head) :- Body)` parses as a `:-`/2 term, not as a
  clause. The query then fails, `.once()` returns `false`, and a probe that stringifies
  the missing binding reports `"undefined"` rather than an error — MAIN's first P4/P6 run
  was vacuous this way and read as a pass. Assert `assertz((Head :- Body))`, and make the
  probe's `once` throw on failure.
