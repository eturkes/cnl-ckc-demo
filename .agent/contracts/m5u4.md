Closes S2 on the proof surface, S3, S3b+, S3c+. Depends u3.
Split seam: typed proof branches ↔ the raw source-head join.

## What ships today (measured)

u3 made the ANSWER real. The PROOF is still fabricated:

```prolog
derive(clinical_advice(Q,Source,Answer),_,_,P,proved) :- !,
  clinical_advice(Q,Source,Answer), clinical_advice_source(Q,Source,Answer,Sites),
  advice_nodes(Sites,P).
advice_nodes([site(L,H)|T],[node(line(L),H,[])|R]) :- advice_nodes(T,R).
```

- `clinical_advice_source/4` = 12 emitted facts carrying the precomputed answer term and a
  48-entry `site(Line,Head)` list — one site per selected content sentence, NOT the 686.
- Every fabricated node has an EMPTY child list, so the ladder draws a flat row of raw
  compiled heads and no derivation ever ran. `clinical_advice_source/4` is also the last
  precomputed answer statement reachable in the image.
- `src/engine/session.ts` `#proofSteps` DROPS `naf/1` silently and throws on any node that
  is not `node/3` — the shape `derive/5` already produces for a real derivation.

## The change

**One clause carries the answer and its proof, so they cannot diverge.**

`tools/kb/clinical.mjs`: `clinical_advice/3` becomes a delegation to a new `clinical_advice/4`
whose fourth argument is the proof. The assembly body moves to `/4` unchanged — same gate
count equality, same `clinical_groups/2` — but it collects `Rule-Proof` pairs from
`clinical_derive/4` instead of discarding the proof.

```prolog
clinical_advice(Q,S,A) :- clinical_advice(Q,S,A,_).
clinical_advice(Q,'$guideline_id'(product,Doc,First,ref(1),[]),
                clinical_answer(Doc,Groups,Passage),Proof) :- …
  findall(R-P,clinical_derive(Doc,_,R,P),Derived), length(Derived,N),
  clinical_split(Derived,Rules,Proofs), clinical_groups(Rules,Groups),
  clinical_join(Proofs,Proof).
```

`tools/kb/proof.mjs`: `advice_nodes/2` is deleted and the arm becomes

```prolog
derive(clinical_advice(Q,Source,Answer),_,_,P,proved) :- !, clinical_advice(Q,Source,Answer,P).
```

`clinical_advice_source/4` and its `discontiguous` directive stop being emitted.

`src/engine/protocol.ts`: `ProofStep` becomes a discriminated union.

| kind | fields | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `clause` | `line`, `head`, `predicate`, `document?`, `sentence?`, `children` | resolved against a compiled clause at that line |
| `assumption` | `head`, `predicate` | query-local hypothetical; NO line — nothing in the KB asserts it |
| `negation` | `goal` | `\+` succeeded; a proof of absence, not a source clause |

`session.ts` decodes all three; the ladder narrows on `kind`.
