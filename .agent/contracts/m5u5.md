## What ships today

u4 delivers assumption and negation steps typed and complete, and the ladder narrows to
`proofClauses` — so the two arms that carry no source line are decoded and then never shown.
A reader sees 9–144 clause rows and no sign that the derivation was granted anything.

## The change

Dedup is per PROOF, not per rung. 3,930 leaves collapse to the document's own premise set —
4 to 131 rows — and that set is shown once inside the EXISTING `Live Prolog proof` rung,
beside the clause list. No ladder step is added and no `<ol>` item moves.

- A premise row carries a hypothetical badge and **no line**. A line would claim the
  guideline states it.
- The one NAF goal renders in its own block with its own badge: proved absent is not the same
  claim as granted.
- Graph focus stays clause-only. `proofSourceLines`, `sentences` and `documentId` already
  derive from `proofClauses`, and nothing here may widen them.

## Out of scope

The premise literal renders as canonical Prolog, skolem and all
(`'$clinical_hypothetical'('cdc2022-opioid-rec01',2,0)`). That is the same register as every
other engine-authored value in this UI. u14 owns the surrounding claim copy.
