# u3 — runtime `clinical_advice` assembly · kernel

Closes S1. Depends u2. Split seam: `session.ts` evaluator plumbing ↔ `questions/` assembly.

## What ships today (measured, `tools/kb/clinical.mjs` + `tools/kb/proof.mjs`)

The catalog goal is `clinical_advice('<qid>',Source,Answer)` for all 7 questions, and
`clinical_advice/3` is **12 stored facts** — one per (question, document) selection. The
answer is looked up, never derived. That is S1.

| emission | count | site | shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `clinical_advice/3` | 12 facts | `clinical.mjs:697` | `(QId, SourceId, Statement)` |
| `clinical_advice_source/4` | 12 facts | `:698` | `(QId, SourceId, Statement, [site(L,H)…])` |
| `clinical_rule/3` | 48 facts | `:681` | `(Doc, S, rule(Conds,Subj,Mode,Actions))` |
| `clinical_gate/4` | 48 rules | `:676` | body = `clinical_use(L,H)`×N, then `clinical_rule/3` |
| `clinical_premise/4` | 346 facts | `:687` | `(Doc, S, N, Literal)` |

- `SourceId` = `'$guideline_id'(product,Doc,FirstContentSentence,ref(1),[])`.
- `Statement` = `clinical_answer(Doc,[rule(…)…],Passage)`; `Passage` is the aligned English
  prose off the provenance model (`passages.get(document)`), not ACE.
- The 48 per-sentence `clinical_rule/3` reassemble into the 38 shipped group terms by
  consequent: merge on identical `(Subject,Mode,Actions)`, concatenate conditions in
  sentence order, group order = first appearance. Byte-identical, 0 divergences (u1).
- u2 ships `clinical_derive(Doc,S,Rule,P)` at `clinical_depth(2)`, which enumerates
  `clause(clinical_gate(Doc,S,_,_),Body,_)` in emission order, reads the cited heads out of
  that stored body, asserts nothing, and binds `Rule` only after `derive_all/4` succeeds.

## The change

`clinical_advice/3` becomes ONE rule that assembles each answer from `clinical_derive/4`.
Two new build-time emissions carry what is genuinely data rather than inference product:

- `clinical_source(QId, Doc, FirstSentence)` — 12 facts, question→document selection order.
- `clinical_passage(Doc, Passage)` — 12 facts, the aligned source passage.

Merging is builtin-only (`app/3` already exists in `PROOF_SOURCE`; the closed image has no
`library(lists)` — no `append/3`, `member/2`, `maplist/2`).

## Acceptance predicates

| id | predicate | how it is decided |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | All 12 answers derive: `clinical_advice(Q,S,A)` yields exactly 12 solutions over the shipped image | live query, node project |
| A2 | Every derived `Answer` is byte-identical to u1's `artifacts.answers` oracle, in raw contribution order | rendered-term compare, 12/12 |
| A3 | Every derived `Source` is byte-identical to the retired fact's `SourceId` | same |
| A4 | Zero `clinical_advice/3` **facts** remain in the payload; the only clause is the rule | helper-source scan |
| A5 | `clinical_advice/3` is **static**: `assertz(clinical_advice(…))` raises a permission error | live `catch/3` inside Prolog |
| A6 | All 48 sentences participate — withholding any one document's premises drops exactly that document's answer | premise-erasure control, 12→11 |
| A7 | Erasing any one of the 686 cited clause lines fails its sentence and therefore its document's answer | reuses u2's 686-site campaign shape, sampled |
| A8 | The 38 group terms reassemble in Prolog exactly as u1 reassembles them in JS | differential over 12 documents |
| A9 | `pnpm gate` rc 0, and the answer bytes reaching the browser are unchanged | gate + `serializeAnswer` compare |
| A10 | An answer is all-or-nothing: a document with any underivable sentence yields NO answer, never a truncated one | A6's control, which found the defect |

**A10 was not in the first draft, and A6 is what found it.** `findall/3` alone collects
whatever happens to derive, so withholding one document's premises left its other sentences
proving and shipped a silently truncated answer that still read as complete. The rule now
counts that document's gates and demands the same number of derivations. Every erasure
control depends on this: without it, a missing premise or an erased cited clause shrinks an
answer instead of removing it, and the control reads green.

  from `clinical_advice(` and `clinical_advice_source(` lines. With the facts gone its base

  stays green while the answer bypasses the KB. A5 makes the consult fail. Replace it with a
  `guideline_*` perturbation that also moves the line-keyed proof. u7 owns the full campaign;
  u3 owns this one case.
- `tools/kb/catalog.mjs:5` says goals "query the `clinical_advice/3` facts compiled into the
  PVM". False after this unit.
- `tests/clinical-records.test.ts` T9 freezes `src/questions/{advice,serialize,humanize}.ts`
  and `tools/kb/provenance.mjs` byte-for-byte at `22053ef`, and span-hashes `clinical.mjs`'s
  `modifierTerm`/`actionTerm`/`groupTerm`/`answerTerm`. **None of those may move.**
  `answerTerm` keeps its build-time role as the ORACLE; it just stops being emitted as a fact.

## Out of scope

`clinical_advice_source/4` and the fabricated proof dispatch
(`proof.mjs`: `derive(clinical_advice(…),…) :- !, clinical_advice(…), clinical_advice_source(…)`)
are **u4**. Until u4 lands, the precomputed statement is still reachable in the image under
`clinical_advice_source/4` — the answer PATH is real inference from u3, the proof path is not.
Removing the cut spans u3+u4; alone it yields 0/7 proofs.
