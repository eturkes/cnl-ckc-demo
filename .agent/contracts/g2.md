Binds `.agent/spec.md` `Decisions` (clinical concept network rulings; IMPLEMENT orientation
rulings) and `.agent/contracts/gi-orientation.md` R1–R9. Revision 3: rev. 2 answered consultant-5's review

census inside this unit and reviewed by a `consultant` before the projection code uses them.

## Terms

- **Frame** (g1): one sentence variant — antecedent literals with their contexts (`actual`, a
  body box bound by `guideline_operator(Outer,V,Op)`, or inside `\+`), consequent heads, boxes
  `box(B)` with outer + operator, guard literals apart.
- **Concept** = one KB entity noun across the corpus, readable form = hyphens → spaces. Not
  concepts: `clinician` (the generic actor) and the bare `patient`, `benefit`, `risk`.
- **Negated literal** = inside `\+ (…)` or in a context under a `-` box.
- **Main event** = a consequent event that does not describe a participant of another consequent
  event. Event E is EMBEDDED in event P (a relative clause: `a clinician that does not rapidly
  taper`, `a level of care that a patient can possibly need`) when E's subject is not P's subject
  and either — same context — E's SUBJECT is argument 2+ or a phrase object of P, or — P's
  context a strict ancestor of E's — any referent of E is argument 2+ or a phrase object of P.
  Embedding is acyclic and every embedded event reaches a main event; a sentence whose events
  form a cycle falls back whole to a `statement`. Embedded events never form their own reading;
  they stay in their parent's payload (re-review: 20 embeddings, 0 cycles, `rec05-imp25` S7,
  `s60-07` S2 and `s20-13` S2's two-step chain caught; `s18-07` S1 keeps `misapply` main).
- **Occurrence** = one main event of one frame; key `document:sentence#variant@event-ref`.
- **Shared scope**: a `-` box (negation) holding two or more main events, or a `may`/`can` box
  holding two or more main events, is read as one joint claim — negation and possibility do not
  distribute over a conjunction; `should`/`must` do.

## Roles of an occurrence

- **Reading** = chain (operator walk to `actual`, outermost first: `should`, `should not` =
  `should`>`-`, `may`, `can`, `must`, root `-` = `it is false that`) + qualifier words + verb (+ a
  property and its preposition under T2), each word once. A fact reads the verb's finite form,
  which must occur in the sentence's own ACE text, else the build refuses the sentence by name.
- **Actor** = argument 1's referent.
- **Target**, first rule that applies: T1 argument 2 is an entity → its noun. T2 argument 2 is a
  property and the event has a `for` phrase → that phrase's noun. T3 no argument 2, and (verb,
  preposition) is in ⟨complement table⟩ → that phrase's noun. Else none. A duration, quantity or
  time frame is never a target.
- **Payload** = every other consequent literal of the occurrence, with its attachment and scope:
  higher arguments, phrases, properties, non-default cardinalities + units, time annotations,
  preferences, and the embedded events beneath it. Nothing in the consequent is dropped.
- **Sources**, first rule that applies:
  - S1 the actor is a concept → `[actor]`.
  - S2 else the starts: argument-2 concepts of condition events in context `actual` and not
    negated, in body order — first events whose subject is a ⟨patient-role noun⟩ (situations; the
    table censuses the subjects of every condition verb, so `newborn` joins),
    then events whose verb is `start`, `continue`, `prescribe`, `give` or `receive` (treatment in
    course) — minus the target, deduplicated.
  - S3 else the event's `for` phrase noun, only when that noun is a ⟨situation noun⟩: a concept
    the KB states a patient-role referent has (`have` with a patient-role subject, anywhere in the
    corpus), or one the sentence's own condition states something is `appropriate for` (`If a
    nonopioid pharmacologic therapy is appropriate for a specific condition then …`). Durations,
    purposes and beneficiaries never qualify.
  - S4 else none.

## Coverage + buckets

- **Sentence coverage**: every sentence of the bag has exactly one coverage record listing its
  variants and occurrences — counted against an independent sentence enumeration over the clause
  sites, never g1's frames.
- **Sentence-level kinds** (the whole sentence): `exclusion` (carries `guideline_exclusion/1`
  heads → the boundaries view: its condition concepts → the concept `recommendation`,
  `recommendations do not apply`; deduplicated by clause body up to variable renaming), `grade`
  (a recommendation document's grade copula → the document's grade annotation), `eventless`
  (`There is no …` → a statement on every concept it names, else on its document).
- **Occurrence buckets**, exactly one per occurrence, `shared` taking precedence over `line` and
  `loop`:
  - `example` — the actor and a NONEMPTY set of argument-2 nouns all end `-example` (the KB's own
    term for an example) → listed beside its sentence, searchable by each item.
  - `line` — sources non-empty, target a concept, source ≠ target → one line per source; all lines
    of one occurrence share its key, full condition, payload and rule id.
  - `loop` — exactly one concept among sources ∪ target, or source = target, or several sources
    with no concept target (one loop per source, each marked as the same rule) → the user's loop.
  - `shared` — the occurrence sits in a shared scope → one statement carrying the whole joint
    claim, attached to every concept its main events name.
  - `statement` — no concept at either end → a statement on every concept its sentence names, else
    on its document.
- **Metadata**, orthogonal to the bucket: supersession record (law, URL, date), scope guard,
  supersession guard, document kind (coverage section).

## Line identity

A canvas line merges occurrences only when their complete semantic readings are equal: source,
target, reading, payload, condition, document kind, guard structure and supersession state. Each
line lists every occurrence it merges.

## Asset envelope

`schemaVersion`; the source-input digest (bag input digest, producer + table digests); the
occurrence → clause-site map (document, sentence, line) the trace joins against live proof lines;
the dependency closure the layout adds in g4.

## Predicates (suites `tests/network-*.test.ts`; each with a planted firing witness)

- N1 sentence coverage: every sentence has one record; the independent enumeration and the
  records agree in count and in ids. Witness: one sentence dropped from the producer's input.
- N2 every main event is exactly one occurrence in exactly one bucket; every embedded event sits
  in its parent's payload. Witnesses: `rec05-imp25` S7 and `s60-07` S2 (no tapering line; no
  `patient can need` line); an event dropped.
- N3 reading ≡ chain for lines, loops and statements alike. Witness: `should`>`-` rendered
  `should`.
- N4 no source from a negated literal. Witnesses: a `-` box payload and a `\+` payload.
- N5 roles reproduce a role oracle — a second mapping, sharing no emit code, that reads T1–T3 and
  S1–S4 off the frames — for every occurrence. Witness: S3 admitting a duration (`s47-16`).
- N6 payload completeness: payload ∪ consumed literals = the occurrence's consequent literals.
  Witness: one phrase dropped.
- N7 shared scope: no `-`/`may`/`can` box with two main events yields per-event lines. Witness: a
  constructed `it is false that X and Y`.
- N8 identity: two readings equal but for supersession state, or guard, stay two lines (constructed
  regression); merged lines list all their occurrences.
- N9 records: 12 exclusion records → 8 conditions; every supersession record and guard projects as
  metadata; no evaluation-date exemption (n30 Accept, moved from A1).
- N10 examples never become nodes or lines; each is reachable from its sentence and by each item.
- N11 condition text = the sentence's own ACE text (Decisions); no reduced span in this unit.
- N12 the declared tables are total over their census: every (verb, preposition) of a no-argument-2
  main event and every subject noun of a condition `have`/`receive`/`take` event is either in its
  table or in the table's recorded exclusions.
