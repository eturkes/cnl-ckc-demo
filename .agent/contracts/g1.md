# g1 — sentence frames (kernel)

`tools/kb/frames.mjs`: the compiled bag's clause sites → one frame per sentence variant, the
structural input of g2's projection (`.agent/contracts/g2.md` Terms). Tier `kernel`: g2's role
oracle and its independent sentence enumeration re-read the same sites, but a frame that drops or
mis-scopes a literal would reach both, so the frames carry their own adversarial suite.

## Inputs

`parseClauseSites(files)` (`tools/kb/provenance.mjs`) — every clause site with its line,
document, sentence and source text. Its `body` flattens `\+`, so frames re-read the text.

## Reader

`readClause(text)` → `{ head: Literal, body: Item[] }`; `Item` = `{ literal }` | `{ naf: Item[] }`.
A literal = `{ name, args }`, args as source text; `'$guideline_id'(Role,Doc,S,ref(N)|box(N),
Deps)` decodes to `{ role, document, sentence, ref | box, deps[] }`. The reader admits the
corpus's clause syntax alone — `Head.` or `Head :- Item, …, Item.` with `\+ Literal` and
`\+ (Items)` — and refuses anything else by line.

## Frames

- **Variant**: the clauses of one sentence whose bodies, after the guard literals are set aside
  and variables renamed canonically by first occurrence, are identical; variants number 1, 2, … by
  first appearance.
- **Guards**, set aside per clause and recorded on the frame: the scope guard `\+
  guideline_exclusion(K)` and the supersession guard `guideline_evaluation_date(V),
  guideline_satisfies(exactly,V,less,Date)`.
- **Records**, attached to the sentence whatever their body: `guideline_supersession/4` facts and
  `guideline_exclusion/1` heads.
- **Referents**: a consequent referent → `ref(N)`; a box → `box(B)`; an antecedent top-level
  referent → `dep(k)`, its position in the head's Skolem `Deps`; a box-local existential keeps its
  canonical variable.
- **Boxes**: each `guideline_operator(Outer, box(B), Op)` head → `{ outer, operator }`; chain =
  walk to `actual`, outermost first. Body boxes: `guideline_operator(actual, V, Op)` in a body
  binds V; a body literal whose context is V is under Op.

## Predicates (suite `tests/frames.test.ts`, node project, the verified bag)

- F1 partition: every clause site that carries a sentence lands in exactly one frame or one
  sentence record; re-collecting the frames gives back the site list exactly.
- F2 round trip: each frame clause's head + canonical body with its guards restored equals the
  site's own text up to variable renaming (alpha-equivalence), all 18,845 sites.
- F3 variants: a sentence holds as many frames as distinct canonical antecedents; census = the
  sentences with more than one, named.
- F4 scope: every head context resolves to `actual` or a box declared in its frame; every chain
  ends at `actual`.
- F5 negation kept: the count of `\+` in the source text equals the frame's NAF items + scope
  guards; a body literal under a negation box reports that box.
- F6 reader property: for generated clauses over the corpus syntax (literals, nested NAF, quoted
  atoms with doubled quotes, `'$guideline_id'` terms, lists), `readClause(render(c))` = `c`; the
  generator's seed is fixed (`tests/support/property.ts` `holds`); the reader refuses a planted
  operator, a missing period and an unbalanced parenthesis by line.
- F7 census in `tests/census.test.ts`: sentences, frames, multi-variant sentences, consequent
  events, records — derived, never transcribed.

Each predicate is seen red on a planted wrong reader or input before it counts (commit body).
