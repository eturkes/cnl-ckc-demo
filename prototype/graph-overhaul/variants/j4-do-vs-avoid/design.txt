premise = patient situation → recommended actions against actions to avoid; exact condition oppositions expose a decision hinge, never imply one recommendation cancels another.
form = split network: situation rail → positive and negative branches; expandable edge buttons retain conditions and targets. Conditions that change advice sit between the rails.
node = each source/target concept in g.network; visual occurrences repeat a concept per action to keep the relationship legible.
edge = each g.network action; facts/capabilities remain labelled as such, not recast as recommendations. Overview also shows generic-source negative edges from the same recommendation as an active situation edge, explicitly labelled same recommendation / different condition.
hinge = two same-document clinical rules whose whole conditions become identical after replacing 'does not outweigh' with 'outweighs', and whose unmodified conditions differ; both entire conditions + actions shown.
model source = g.network (network.mjs heuristic over clinical parse), g.rules for verbatim conditions; g.documents for sources; g.questions for offline answer trace.
hand-made: do/avoid columns = prototype reading of positive/negative action polarity, not KB categories; default situation opioid therapy; no hand-picked clinical pair or treatment taxonomy.
